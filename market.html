Sources & Methodology

How this page works

This page is updated manually, roughly weekly, using the primary and secondary sources credited on each card above — including Freddie Mac, FHFA, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, the U.S. Energy Information Administration, the U.S. Census Bureau, Redfin, Realtor.com, Insurify, Zonda, and Angi.

Project costs are estimates only. Actual costs vary significantly by home, materials, contractor, and local market — treat every range here as a starting point for a conversation with a contractor, not a quote.

Rates, prices, and costs change. The date shown on each card reflects when that figure was published or last verified, not necessarily today.
