Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ROQ Innovation LLC ("ROQ", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the ROQ mobile application. By using ROQ, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly: your home name, address, projects, experiences, documents, and account information. We also collect usage data, device information, and pulse history derived from your activity.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and personalize the ROQ experience, power SLADE (our AI home advisor), send notifications, analyze and improve the app, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations.

3. SLADE AI Advisor

SLADE is powered by the Claude API provided by Anthropic, PBC. When you interact with SLADE, your messages and relevant home context are sent to Anthropic's API to generate responses. Anthropic's data handling is governed by their own privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy. We do not sell your data to Anthropic or any third party.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a trusted cloud infrastructure provider. We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit and at rest.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only with trusted service providers (such as Supabase and Anthropic), when required by law, in connection with a business transfer, or with your explicit consent.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may access, update, or delete your home data at any time through the app. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly within the app, under Settings. This deletes your home profile, projects, tasks, memories, documents, contacts, ROQ content, and SLADE conversation history immediately and cannot be undone. California and EEA residents may have additional rights under applicable law.

7. Children's Privacy

ROQ is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
