About ROQ Home

What is ROQ Home?

ROQ Home helps homeowners make better home decisions. It brings together three things: the ROQ app for managing the home you own, the Dream Home Plan for planning a new build, and renovation planning for the projects in between.

Who is behind ROQ?

ROQ was started by a young Kansas couple who had a vision to empower homeowners to make better home decisions. The company is ROQ Innovation LLC, based in Topeka, Kansas. You can read more on the Story page.

Is ROQ Home the same as ROQ International or ROQ.US screen printing?

No. ROQ Home (roqhome.com), made by ROQ Innovation LLC, is for homeowners and is unrelated to ROQ International or ROQ.US, which manufacture screen printing equipment.

The ROQ app

What does the ROQ app do?

The ROQ app puts your entire home in one place: its health, its projects, its story, its worth, and an AI advisor that knows your specific house. See it on the App page.

Where can I get the ROQ app?

The ROQ app is available for iPhone and is free to download on the App Store.

Dream Home Plan

What is a Dream Home Plan?

A Dream Home Plan is ROQ’s planning package for a new home. It covers site feasibility and planning, dimensioned floor plans and layout, architectural drawings and CAD files, design, materials and buildability, and professional handoff documentation.

Is ROQ a licensed architect or engineering firm?

No. ROQ Innovation LLC is not a licensed architectural or engineering firm. Our home concepts, floor plans, renderings, and site feasibility assessments are intended for conceptual planning, visualization, and informational purposes. ROQ does not provide stamped architectural drawings, certified engineering plans, or construction-ready documents.

Can I build directly from a Dream Home Plan?

Not on its own. Before construction, all designs must undergo applicable professional review and obtain any required permits, approvals, and architectural or engineering certifications. The handoff documentation is there to make that step with your licensed professionals easier.

How do I get started on a Dream Home Plan?

Go to the Designs page and choose Get started. Tell us where you are considering your build (an address, a location, or a lot) and the best way to reach you, and we will follow up about your project.

Renovation planning

What does ROQ renovation planning include?

Renovation planning includes custom design concepts, a detailed project budget, material recommendations, neighborhood value and ROI analysis, DIY vs contractor guidance, and a clear timeline and roadmap.

How accurate are ROQ’s cost and value estimates?

They are estimates only. Figures may be generated using available market information, the details you provide, third-party data, or automated calculations. They are not official quotes or appraisals, and actual costs can differ based on location, market conditions, contractors, materials, and the condition of the property. Verify estimates with appropriate professionals before making financial or construction decisions.

How do I start planning a renovation?

Go to the Projects page and choose Start planning. Tell us what you are working on, such as a kitchen, bathroom, or whole home, and whether you would like us to reach you by email or phone.
