FAQ

Common questions.

Is FIELDSTONE a final, permit-ready construction set?

No. FIELDSTONE is a Concept Edition design book. Plans show design intent and may require adaptation to local codes, site conditions, and engineering. Site-specific permitting, structural, civil, MEP engineering, surveys, and professional seals are provided locally as required.

Can the dimensions or square footage change?

Dimensions are to face of exterior walls and centerline of interior walls (architectural). Exterior dimensions and square footage may vary slightly based on final wall assemblies and local requirements.

How is FIELDSTONE priced?

FIELDSTONE is premium through design quality and material control — not uncontrolled scope. The design package is priced separately from construction; construction costs are established with your local builder. Request pricing to start that process.

Can I customize the plan for my site or preferences?

Yes. FIELDSTONE includes ten controlled architectural options — such as a 3-car garage expansion, basement foundation, or bonus room — and is designed for regional adaptation without changing its identity.

Where can FIELDSTONE be built?

FIELDSTONE is designed for regional adaptation — materials, performance assemblies, and code details are coordinated to your specific climate, site, and local builder, while the architectural identity stays the same.
