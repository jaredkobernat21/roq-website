FIELDSTONE Design Philosophy

"Traditional form. Modern proportion. Natural materials. Quiet luxury. Rural practicality."

FIELDSTONE is designed as a real home first: simple gabled forms, a deep porch, a restrained material palette, logical circulation, and a compact second level.

The visual character comes from proportion and material quality rather than decoration.

Planning Logic

Three routes define the house.

Arrival

Garage → Mudroom → Pantry → Kitchen

Groceries and daily entry stay out of the formal living space.

Owner

Primary Bedroom → Bath → Closet → Laundry

Private circulation is short, useful, and independent.

Guest

Porch → Foyer → Great Room → Dining → Rear Porch

The home opens gradually toward the landscape. Targeted main-floor public rooms stay generous without becoming oversized.

03 · Material Language

Fewer materials, better proportion, warmer tones.

Nothing stark, nothing arbitrary. Natural, timeless, warm, and refined — designed to feel collected and connected to the landscape.

  • Materials change at architectural edges.
  • Stone terminates at real corners and returns.
  • Wood remains neutral — never orange.
  • Bronze replaces harsh pure black.
  • Texture changes more than color.
  • Regional substitutions preserve the same character.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is FIELDSTONE a final, permit-ready construction set?

No. FIELDSTONE is a Concept Edition design book. Plans show design intent and may require adaptation to local codes, site conditions, and engineering — final construction documents will include structural, MEP, and code-specific information. Site-specific permitting, structural, civil, MEP engineering, surveys, and professional seals are provided locally as required.

Can the dimensions or square footage change?

Dimensions are to face of exterior walls and centerline of interior walls (architectural). Exterior dimensions and square footage may vary slightly based on final wall assemblies and local requirements.

What's included in the FIELDSTONE Package?

Dimensioned plans, elevations, sections, kitchen/bath/millwork design, an interior finish and material guide, a visualization set, a builder pricing package, coordinated CAD/BIM source, and the buyer-facing design book — see "What You Receive" above for the full list.

How is FIELDSTONE priced?

FIELDSTONE is premium through design quality and material control — not uncontrolled scope. The design package is priced separately from construction; construction costs are established with your local builder, since they reflect local labor, material availability, climate, code, soils, utilities, and site access. Can I customize the plan for my site or preferences?

Yes. FIELDSTONE includes ten controlled architectural options — such as a 3-car garage expansion, basement foundation, or bonus room — and is designed for regional adaptation without changing its identity.

Where can FIELDSTONE be built?
