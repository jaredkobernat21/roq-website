FAQ

Is this just another lead list?

No. We don't sell a static database. Every search runs against your specific buy box, and we send a curated batch instead of a dump you have to filter yourself.

What markets do you cover?

We're expanding market by market as we onboard investors. Tell us your target market when you build your buy box and we'll confirm coverage.

How often do I get opportunities?

Matches are delivered weekly via email and the ROQ portal, with more context sent as it becomes available between cycles.

What does it cost?

We're currently working with a small group of investors as we expand ROQ. Reach out and we'll walk you through pricing for your buy box.

Do signals guarantee a motivated seller?

No. A signal doesn't mean an owner is necessarily motivated to sell — it tells us where there may be a reason to look closer.
