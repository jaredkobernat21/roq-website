Built differently.

ROQ was founded in Topeka, Kansas around a simple question:

What if understanding real estate didn’t require digging through dozens of disconnected sources?

What started with tools for understanding individual homes has expanded into a broader mission: making the information surrounding homes, property and development easier to understand and act on.
