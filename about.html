You deserve to feel like an owner, not a bystander.

Buying a home or starting a business is supposed to feel like an accomplishment. For a lot of people, it feels like the moment everyone else starts making money off you.

Contractors quote whatever they think you’ll pay. Agents disappear until it’s time to sell again. Software vendors and marketing agencies nickel-and-dime you for tools that should just work.

You shouldn’t need an industry to translate your own home—or your own business—back to you.

ROQ is built to put that understanding back in your hands.

Built differently.

ROQ was founded in Topeka, Kansas around a simple, personal question:

Why does buying a home feel like the last time you’re in control of it?

What started as a way to help one family understand their own home has grown into something bigger: ROQ Home for homeowners, and ROQ Business for the local businesses that keep communities running—built so owners never have to feel like an outsider in what they built again.

The future of ownership belongs to people who understand what they own.

We’re building the tools to help you get there—at home, and in business.
