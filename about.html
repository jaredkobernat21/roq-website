You deserve to feel like a homeowner, not a bystander.

Buying a home is supposed to feel like an accomplishment. For a lot of people, it feels like the moment everyone else starts making money off you.

Contractors quote whatever they think you’ll pay. Agents disappear until it’s time to sell again. Lenders and insurers benefit when you don’t ask questions.

You shouldn’t need an industry to translate your own home back to you.

ROQ is built to put that understanding back in your hands.

How ROQ helps

Understand your home

See what you own, what it’s worth and what it needs—in plain language, not paperwork.

Plan what’s next

Whether it’s a repair, a remodel, or building on land you already own—know your options before you commit.

Move with confidence

Make decisions knowing what they’ll actually cost—not what someone else decides to charge you.

Built differently.

ROQ was founded in Topeka, Kansas around a simple, personal question:

Why does buying a home feel like the last time you’re in control of it?

What started as a way to help one family understand their own home has grown into something bigger: an app, and a build-plan service, built so homeowners never have to feel like an outsider in their own investment again.

The future of homeownership belongs to people who understand what they own.

We’re building the app and the build plans to help you get there.
